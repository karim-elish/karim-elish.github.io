August 2026 · SANER 2027

ASSURE-AI workshop accepted at SANER 2027

NEXUS Lab is pleased to share that the International Workshop on Security Assurance for AI-Assisted Software Engineering (ASSURE-AI) has been accepted for SANER 2027. The workshop will bring together researchers and practitioners working on evidence-based security assurance, false assurance, secure AI-assisted development, and agentic software-engineering workflows.

August 2026 · Research Tool

SecAIDev research workbench now available

SecAIDev provides an interactive workbench for examining security assurance in agent-authored pull requests, including security exposure, potential risk, mitigation evidence, review quality, and potential false assurance.

June 2026 · ICSA

Security-aware architectural smells in Android applications

Congratulations to Mohamed Sylla on our ICSA 2026 paper with Karim Elish, which rethinks MVC violations as security-aware architectural smells in Android applications. The paper was also recognized as a Distinguished Paper Award Candidate.

June 2026 · IWSPA / CODASPY

STALKER studies LLM-amplified behavioral leakage

Congratulations to the STALKER authors—Anastasiya Dmytryk, Sami El-Yaagoubi, Lucas Batista, Philipp Kouterguine, Samuel Gerken, Abdulaziz Alhamadani, and Karim Elish—on the paper. The work examines how composed quasi-identifiers and LLM-assisted inference can amplify behavioral leakage in anonymized marketplace data.

Paper
May 2026 · ICST

Evaluating LLMs for Android bug reproduction

Congratulations to Dennis Carey and co-authors Karim Elish and Paniz Abedin on their ICST 2026 paper investigating whether large language models can faithfully reproduce Android application bugs from natural-language reports. The study introduces intent-aware evaluation through critical-step coverage.

Paper
April 2026 · MSR

AndroMetric and SMELLDroid presented at MSR 2026

Congratulations to Sebastian Siedler and Joyce Champie, together with their co-authors, on presenting two NEXUS datasets in the MSR 2026 Data and Tool Showcase. AndroMetric connects multi-dimensional software metrics with mobile application security, while SMELLDroid supports large-scale research on Android code smells and software quality.

April 2026 · FORGE

MIRROR dataset presented at FORGE 2026

Congratulations to Sebastian Siedler and co-author Karim Elish on the MIRROR paper and dataset. MIRROR provides structural metrics for original and repackaged Android applications to support research on repackaging, structural similarity, and software reuse.

Paper MIRROR artifact
April 2026 · IEEE Access

Systematic review of LLMs for malware detection

Congratulations to Joyce Champie and co-author Karim Elish on their IEEE Access article. The review synthesizes the emerging literature on large language models for malware detection, develops a taxonomy of current approaches, and identifies open research challenges.
