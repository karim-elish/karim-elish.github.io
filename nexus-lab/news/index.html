News & Highlights

Recent publications, datasets, and research milestones from the lab.

June 2026 · ICSA

Security-aware architectural smells in Android applications

Mohamed Sylla and Karim Elish presented work rethinking MVC violations as security-aware architectural smells at IEEE ICSA. The paper was a Distinguished Paper Award Candidate.

June 2026 · IWSPA / CODASPY

LLM-amplified behavioral leakage in anonymized data

STALKER examines composed quasi-identifiers and the ways LLMs can amplify behavioral leakage in anonymized marketplace data.

May 2026 · ICST

Can LLMs reproduce Android application bugs?

Dennis Carey, Karim Elish, and Paniz Abedin investigated automated Android bug reproduction with large language models at IEEE ICST.

April 2026 · MSR

AndroMetric and SMELLDroid at MSR 2026

Two NEXUS datasets were presented in the IEEE/ACM MSR Data and Tool Showcase: AndroMetric and SMELLDroid.

April 2026 · FORGE

MIRROR dataset presented at FORGE

MIRROR provides structural metrics for original and repackaged Android applications to support reuse and repackaging research.

April 2026 · IEEE Access

Systematic review of LLMs for malware detection

A new review develops a taxonomy of large language models for malware detection and identifies open research challenges.
