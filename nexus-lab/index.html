Secure, Trustworthy, and Intelligent Software Engineering Lab

Advancing secure, trustworthy, and intelligent software

NEXUS Lab is a research lab in Lakeland, FL, affiliated with the Department of Computer Science at Florida Polytechnic University. We study the security, quality, and trustworthiness of modern software systems — and develop empirical and AI-driven methods and tools to understand, evaluate, and improve them.
